## 3. Permitted Uses (No Explicit Permission Required)

You may use the LIOP wordmark without prior written consent in the following situations:

1. **Accurate Origin Attribution**: Stating truthfully that your software implements, connects to, or is compatible with the LIOP protocol (e.g., *"Compatible with LIOP"*, *"Plugin for LIOP Mesh"*, *"Built using LIOP TypeScript SDK"*).
2. **Community Discussion & Educational Content**: Referencing LIOP in articles, tutorials, technical talks, academic papers, and podcasts.
3. **Unmodified Redistribution**: Distributing exact, unmodified copies or binary artifacts built directly from official LIOP source repositories, provided all copyright and `NOTICE` files remain intact.

## 4. Prohibited Uses (Prior Written Permission Required)

You **may not** use LIOP trademarks in any of the following ways without explicit, written authorization from Nekzus Solutions:

1. **Naming Forks or Derivative Products**: You cannot name a fork, modified version, or competing protocol with "LIOP" as the primary name or in a manner that implies official endorsement (e.g., *"LIOP Pro"*, *"LIOP Enterprise"*, *"Official LIOP Server"*). 
   - *Acceptable alternative*: *"Acme Mesh Protocol (based on LIOP source code)"*.
2. **Domain Names & Social Handles**: Registering domain names, npm organization names, or social media handles containing "LIOP" as the dominant element.
3. **Endorsement & Certification**: Claiming or implying that a third-party commercial service, consulting practice, or hardware appliance is *"LIOP Certified"*, *"Endorsed by LIOP"*, or *"Official LIOP Partner"*.
4. **Altered Logos**: Modifying, distorting, recoloring, or combining official LIOP logos with third-party branding.
